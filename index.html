About

A little about me

Hello there, I'm — a BSIT graduate from Davao del Norte State College in the Philippines, with experience in documentation, digital tools and project coordination.

I enjoy turning ideas, workflows, and information into digital experiences that feel clear, useful, and thoughtfully designed.

During my studies and internship, I worked on web and mobile projects, UI/UX design and prototyping, and different forms of documentation. I also had the opportunity to lead a documentation team, where I assigned tasks, tracked progress, created/reviewed documents, and coordinated with team members and our supervisor.

Now that I’ve graduated, I’m looking forward to putting my skills to work—whether that’s helping a team stay organized, preparing documents, coordinating tasks, or handling digital work that helps things keep running smoothly.

I'm especially interested in virtual assistance, administrative and office support, documentation, and legal support where I can apply my organizational, communication, and digital skills in work that requires attention to detail.
